# Release 24 candidate

Status: unpublished

## Included

- Four new researched category catalogs: Portable Speakers, VR Headsets, Wearables, and Game Consoles & Handhelds.
- Ten products and 45 generated pairings per category: 40 products and 180 comparisons after approval.
- Category-specific specification schemas, scoring thresholds, scoring versions, and use-case presets.
- Public category pages and permanent comparison routes for all four categories.
- Public specification tables with field-level manufacturer provenance.
- A six-category home-page comparison switcher.
- Catalog detail support and related-comparison discovery for the new categories.
- Overall, use-case, and feature voting for the new comparison models.
- Batch-level research approval through the existing protected Research Console.

## Safety and integrity

- The release is additive and preserves existing Headphones and Smartphones products, comparisons, votes, offers, analytics, ingestion, and research workflows.
- No research batch publishes automatically. Public database changes still require the owner's explicit batch approval.
- Missing or inapplicable specifications are excluded from scoring.
- Subjective or non-comparable factors are disclosed as limitations rather than represented by invented numbers.
- Every scored field has a manufacturer source and retrieval timestamp.
- Research images use stable manufacturer-hosted assets when available; products without a stable direct asset show an intentional placeholder.

## Activation after deployment

1. Open `/admin/research` as the owner so the four bundled batches are uploaded as pending.
2. Review each batch's evidence summary and generated comparison preview.
3. Approve and publish Portable Speakers, VR Headsets, Wearables, and Game Consoles & Handhelds.
4. Verify ten approved products and 45 live comparisons on each category page.

The full selection and model rationale is in `docs/release-24-remaining-categories-research.md`.
