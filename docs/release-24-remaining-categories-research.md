# Release 24: remaining-category research and comparison models

Research date: September 10, 2026  
Market and configuration: United States; the named current base configuration unless stated otherwise  
Publication status: release candidate only; all four data batches remain unpublished until owner approval

## Outcome

Release 24 expands Bettr Than's research and comparison system to the four remaining launch categories: Portable Speakers, VR Headsets, Wearables, and Game Consoles & Handhelds. Each category contains ten researched products. At publication time, the system generates all 45 unique pairings per category, for 40 products and 180 comparisons total.

Every scored specification has its own manufacturer URL and retrieval timestamp. Missing or inapplicable values are excluded from a score rather than inferred or counted as a loss. Reference prices are launch MSRP or a manufacturer-listed reference price, never presented as a live retailer offer.

The lineups are a practical comparison set, not a claim that one universal top-ten ranking exists. They combine current editorial shortlists with availability, category breadth, distinct use cases, and verifiable first-party specifications. Manufacturer sources control the facts; editorial guides only inform which products merit inclusion.

## Comparison and evidence rules

- Ten products produce `10 × 9 ÷ 2 = 45` unique pairings per category.
- A comparison is publishable only when both products are approved and the selected preset passes that category's minimum shared-field and weighted-coverage thresholds.
- The score is recalculated from verified field provenance and a versioned category model; it is not stored as an opaque editorial number.
- Equal values split the relevant points. Missing values remove that weight from both products.
- Every public comparison exposes its coverage, scored factors, excluded factors, manufacturer links, and the category model's limitations.
- Community voting remains separate from the evidence-based score and supports overall, use-case, and individual-feature dimensions.

## Publication workflow

The four immutable JSON batches are registered with the research console. On the owner's first visit after deployment, the console uploads them as pending batches. One explicit **Approve & publish batch** action per category creates or updates the ten products and generates the 45 eligible comparisons. No individual product review is required, but the public database is not changed until that batch-level approval.
